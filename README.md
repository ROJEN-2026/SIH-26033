## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

FarmLink AI is a digital marketplace that connects farmers and FPOs directly with consumers, retailers, restaurants, wholesalers, and bulk buyers. The platform reduces dependency on multiple intermediaries and improves transparency in agricultural trading.

The platform introduces a Farmer Profit Protection and Collective Selling Engine that calculates the minimum profitable selling price by considering production expenses, transportation costs, and handling charges. Farmers with similar crops and nearby locations can combine their produce to fulfil bulk orders and improve their bargaining power.

Buyers can submit competitive offers for aggregated produce, allowing farmers to compare prices and choose profitable deals. AI-based demand forecasting predicts upcoming crop demand, while smart logistics optimizes transportation routes and delivery schedules. The platform also matches surplus produce with nearby buyers to reduce food wastage.

How it addresses the problem:
- Connects farmers directly with buyers, reducing unnecessary intermediaries.
- Calculates minimum profitable selling prices to help farmers make better selling decisions.
- Combines produce from nearby farmers to improve bargaining power.
- Supports competitive buyer bidding for transparent price discovery.
- Uses demand forecasting to reduce unsold agricultural produce.
- Optimizes delivery routes to reduce transportation costs and consumer prices.
Innovation and uniqueness:
FarmLink AI combines farmer profit protection, collective selling, competitive buyer bidding, surplus produce matching, AI-based demand forecasting, and smart logistics in one platform. Unlike a basic agricultural marketplace, it helps small farmers participate in bulk selling while making informed and profitable decisions.

## Technical Approach

Technologies to be used:
- Frontend: Flutter / React.js
- Backend: Python FastAPI
- Database: PostgreSQL
- AI/ML: Python, Scikit-learn, Pandas
- Demand Forecasting: Machine learning using historical demand and seasonal data
- Route Optimization: OpenStreetMap / Mapping APIs
- Authentication: Firebase Authentication / OTP
- Cloud: Firebase / AWS
- Payments: UPI payment gateway integration
- Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging
- Language Support: Regional language interface
Methodology and process of implementation:
1. Farmers, FPOs, buyers, and transport providers register on the platform.
2. Farmers list their crops, quantity, quality, location, harvest date, and expected price.
3. The Profit Protection Engine calculates the minimum profitable selling price based on expenses and logistics.
4. Nearby farmers with similar crops are grouped together for collective selling.
5. Bulk buyers submit competitive offers for individual or aggregated produce.
6. Farmers compare offers and select suitable selling prices.
7. AI analyses historical sales, seasonal trends, and demand data to forecast crop demand.
8. Smart logistics selects suitable transport and optimizes delivery routes.
9. Buyers track orders, confirm deliveries, and complete digital payments.

## Feasibility and Viability

Analysis of the feasibility of the idea:

FarmLink AI is technically feasible because it uses existing technologies for mobile application development, cloud databases, machine learning, mapping services, and digital payments.

Technical Feasibility: Marketplace, price calculator, farmer aggregation, buyer bidding, forecasting, and logistics modules can be developed using existing frameworks and APIs.

Operational Feasibility: Farmers and FPOs can be onboarded through agricultural associations, village-level coordinators, and local agricultural offices.

Economic Feasibility: Revenue can be generated through small transaction fees, logistics coordination charges, and optional premium analytics for bulk buyers.

Scalability: The platform can initially operate in one district and gradually expand to other regions.

Accessibility: Regional-language support, voice assistance, and a simple interface help farmers with limited digital literacy.

Potential challenges and risks:
- Limited digital literacy among farmers.
- Lack of trust in online transactions.
- Insufficient produce quantities for bulk orders.
- Transportation availability and logistics costs.
- Demand forecasting inaccuracies.
- Quality-related disputes.
- Internet connectivity problems.
- Payment delays.
Strategies for overcoming these challenges:
- Provide simple interfaces, regional languages, and assisted onboarding.
- Use verified profiles, transparent offers, ratings, and transaction records.
- Aggregate produce from nearby farmers.
- Partner with local transport providers.
- Improve forecasting models using verified transaction and market data.
- Use crop images, quality descriptions, and grading information.
- Provide lightweight application features and offline listing support where feasible.
- Integrate secure digital payment systems and transparent payment tracking.
